# MTG Deck Collection Inventory

This document is the authoritative inventory of physical deck-release groups represented in this drawer-face project. Counts use **decks owned / total decks in the release group**.

## Collection and grouping rules

- Keep decks grouped under the physical product or release actually owned.
- Keep Commander Anthology I and Commander Anthology II separate. Do not reassign their reprinted decks to the decks' original Commander releases.
- **Zendikar Rising Commander and Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty Commander share one combined drawer face.** Use the approved vertical split: Zendikar Rising on the left with a `2` marker and Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty on the right with a `2` marker.
- **Commander Legends and Phyrexia: All Will Be One Commander share one combined drawer face.** Use the approved equal vertical split: Commander Legends on the left with a `2` marker and Phyrexia on the right with a `2` marker.
- Commander 2020 / Ikoria and Strixhaven Commander each remain standalone and use a `5` marker.
- **The Brothers' War Commander and Wilds of Eldraine Commander share one combined drawer face.** Use the approved equal vertical split: The Brothers' War on the left with a `2` marker and Wilds of Eldraine on the right with a `2` marker. All owned decks from both releases are represented by this face.
- Both owned Commander Legends decks and both owned Phyrexia decks are represented by their combined face. Their standalone full-release targets remain available as alternatives, not as additional active drawer allocations.
- March of the Machine Commander remains standalone and uses a `5` marker.
- Turtle Power! Commander remains standalone and uses a `1` marker for its single ready-to-play Commander deck.
- Overflow is an open-ended storage category and intentionally uses no count marker.
- Every other release or category remains on its own drawer face unless the user explicitly changes this inventory.
- The deck-count marker shows the number of physical decks represented by that face or panel. It does not show the release's hypothetical complete-set size when the collection is incomplete.
- Secret Lair and Custom Commander Decks have no fixed release total. Their markers remain undetermined until an owned physical-deck count is supplied.

## Current face-card production coverage

Production is complete for every drawer face in this inventory:

- Every finished target and alternate is preserved in its original full-bleed form and also has a separately named active print-margin variant. All active variants use the `_1724x2024_black_margin_1_8in.png` treatment at 600 DPI: a 1644 × 1944 complete-face artwork region, 37-pixel opaque-black side margins, no top margin, a 93-pixel opaque-black bottom margin, and the 3-pixel opaque-black outer cut guide. Superseded 1800 × 2100 white 3/16-inch derivatives are retained only under each set's `src/superseded_white_margin_3_16in/` archive.
- Every fixed-count standalone or residual release has both an unnumbered base and a counted print target using the marker value recorded above.
- Zendikar Rising Commander and Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty Commander have the approved combined vertical-split base and a counted `2 | 2` print target.
- The Brothers' War Commander and Wilds of Eldraine Commander have the approved combined vertical-split base and a counted `2 | 2` print target.
- Commander Legends and Phyrexia: All Will Be One Commander have the approved combined vertical-split base and a counted `2 | 2` print target. Their preserved standalone alternatives each use their full-release count `2`.
- Commander 2020 / Ikoria, Strixhaven Commander, and March of the Machine Commander each have a standalone counted `5` print target.
- Doctor Who Commander has a standalone counted `4` print target representing its four unique release decks.
- Turtle Power! Commander has one standalone counted `1` print target.
- Bloomburrow Commander retains three finished visual candidates, each with a counted `4` sibling.
- Secret Lair and Custom Commander Decks each have one finished unnumbered category face and remain without a count seal until an owned physical-deck count is supplied. Overflow has one finished unnumbered category face and intentionally remains without a count seal by design.

Existing unnumbered targets are retained as non-destructive bases and are not evidence of a missing counted sibling when a counted version is also present.
