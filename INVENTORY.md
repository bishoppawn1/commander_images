# MTG Deck Collection Inventory

This document is the authoritative inventory of physical deck-release groups represented in this drawer-face project. Counts use **decks owned / total decks in the release group**.

## Collection and grouping rules

- Keep decks grouped under the physical product or release actually owned.
- Keep Commander Anthology I and Commander Anthology II separate. Do not reassign their reprinted decks to the decks' original Commander releases.
- **Commander 2013 and Commander 2016 share one combined drawer face.** Use the approved chronological vertical split: Commander 2013 on the left with a `2` marker and Commander 2016 on the right with a `2` marker.
- **Zendikar Rising Commander and Commander Legends share one combined drawer face.** Use the approved chronological vertical split: Zendikar Rising on the left with a `2` marker and Commander Legends on the right with a `2` marker.
- **Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty Commander and The Brothers' War Commander share one combined drawer face.** Use the approved chronological vertical split: Kamigawa on the left with a `2` marker and The Brothers' War on the right with a `2` marker.
- **Phyrexia: All Will Be One Commander and Wilds of Eldraine Commander share one combined drawer face.** Use the approved chronological vertical split: Phyrexia on the left with a `2` marker and Wilds of Eldraine on the right with a `2` marker.
- Commander 2020 / Ikoria and Strixhaven Commander each remain standalone and use a `5` marker.
- All eight releases in the four combined faces are fully represented by their panels. Their standalone full-release targets remain available as alternatives, not as additional active drawer allocations.
- March of the Machine Commander remains standalone and uses a `5` marker.
- Turtle Power! Commander remains standalone and uses a `1` marker for its single ready-to-play Commander deck.
- Overflow is an open-ended storage category and intentionally uses no count marker.
- Every other release or category remains on its own drawer face unless the user explicitly changes this inventory.
- The deck-count marker shows the number of physical decks represented by that face or panel. It does not show the release's hypothetical complete-set size when the collection is incomplete.
- Secret Lair and Custom Commander Decks have no fixed release total. Their markers remain undetermined until an owned physical-deck count is supplied.

### Release-date pairing audit — September 17, 2026

The active inventory, print catalog, combined images, and GIMP arrangement definitions already agree on the four pairs above. For this audit, chronological proximity means minimizing the sum of release-date gaps while using each eligible release exactly once. Eligible releases are Commander products with **two decks owned**, including the partial Commander 2013 and 2016 collections. Unowned releases, Anthology reprints, and the standalone non-Commander Heroes vs. Monsters product are excluded.

| Earlier release | Tabletop release | Later release | Tabletop release | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Commander 2013 | 2013-11-01 | Commander 2016 | 2016-11-11 | 1,106 days |
| Zendikar Rising Commander | 2020-09-25 | Commander Legends | 2020-11-20 | 56 days |
| Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty Commander | 2022-02-18 | The Brothers' War Commander | 2022-11-18 | 273 days |
| Phyrexia: All Will Be One Commander | 2023-02-10 | Wilds of Eldraine Commander | 2023-09-08 | 210 days |

Enumerating all **105** complete pairings confirms that these four consecutive pairs are the **unique minimum**, totaling **1,645 days**. The former arrangement (Commander 2013 / 2016, Zendikar / Kamigawa, Commander Legends / Phyrexia, and Brothers' War / Wilds) totals 2,723 days, or 1,078 days more.

This does not mean every release has its independent closest neighbor: The Brothers' War and Phyrexia are only 84 days apart. Pairing them would leave Kamigawa / Wilds at 567 days; those two pairs would total 651 days instead of the current 483. A one-to-one pairing cannot satisfy every release's independent nearest-neighbor preference. The current arrangement keeps all eight releases paired with the smallest total gap.

Date references: [Commander 2013 and 2016 release table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Magic%3A_The_Gathering_sets), [Zendikar Rising release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/zendikar-rising-release-notes-2020-09-10), [Commander Legends release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-legends-release-notes-2020-11-06), [Kamigawa release announcement](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/card-preview/sagas-kamigawa-neon-dynasty-2022-01-27), [The Brothers' War first look](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/first-look-brothers-war-2022-09-29), [Phyrexia release announcement](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/whats-new-on-the-list-for-phyrexia-all-will-be-one), and [Wilds of Eldraine release announcement](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/on-september-8-be-ready-for-wilds-of-eldraine). ## Current face-card production coverage

Production is complete for every drawer face in this inventory:

- Every finished target and alternate is preserved in its original full-bleed form and has both a canonical `_1724x2024_black_margin_1_8in.png` master at 600 DPI and a `_4138x4858_1440dpi_black_margin_1_8in.png` print sibling at 1440 DPI. The master retains the authoritative 1644 × 1944 complete-face artwork region, 37-pixel opaque-black side margins, no top margin, a 93-pixel opaque-black bottom margin, and the 3-pixel opaque-black outer cut guide; the 1440-DPI sibling is a non-destructive high-quality resampling of the complete composition. Superseded 1800 × 2100 white 3/16-inch derivatives are retained only under each set's `src/superseded_white_margin_3_16in/` archive.
- Every fixed-count standalone or residual release has both an unnumbered base and a counted print target using the marker value recorded above.
- Commander 2013 and Commander 2016 have the approved chronological vertical-split base and a counted `2 | 2` print target.
- Zendikar Rising Commander and Commander Legends have the approved chronological vertical-split base and a counted `2 | 2` print target.
- Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty Commander and The Brothers' War Commander have the approved chronological vertical-split base and a counted `2 | 2` print target.
- Phyrexia: All Will Be One Commander and Wilds of Eldraine Commander have the approved chronological vertical-split base and a counted `2 | 2` print target. Preserved standalone alternatives use their release's marker value.
- Commander 2020 / Ikoria, Strixhaven Commander, and March of the Machine Commander each have a standalone counted `5` print target.
- Doctor Who Commander has a standalone counted `4` print target representing its four unique release decks.
- Turtle Power! Commander has one standalone counted `1` print target.
- Bloomburrow Commander retains three finished visual candidates, each with a counted `4` sibling.
- Secret Lair and Custom Commander Decks each have one finished unnumbered category face and remain without a count seal until an owned physical-deck count is supplied. Overflow has one finished unnumbered category face and intentionally remains without a count seal by design.

Existing unnumbered targets are retained as non-destructive bases and are not evidence of a missing counted sibling when a counted version is also present.
