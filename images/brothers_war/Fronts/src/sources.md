# The Brothers' War Commander source and production record

Downloaded and produced 2026-08-26. All external URLs below are official Wizards of the Coast or Wizards Play Network sources.

## Product, debut, and deck-count verification

- [A First Look at The Brothers' War](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/first-look-brothers-war-2022-09-29) identifies the associated set and its BRC Commander release, previews Urza and Mishra Commander cards, and states that the release has two retro-frame Commander decks.
- [Where to Find The Brothers' War Previews](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/where-to-find-the-brothers-war-previews) is Wizards' official debut/preview hub for the October 27, 2022 set debut and separately lists The Brothers' War Commander previews.
- [The Brothers' War Commander Decklists](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/brothers-war-commander-decklists-2022-10-28) lists exactly two decks: **Urza's Iron Alliance** and **Mishra's Burnished Banner**. This verifies the drawer-face marker value `2`.
- [What's Inside The Brothers' War Boosters?](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/whats-inside-the-brothers-war-boosters) independently states that the release contains two Commander decks, one for each brother, and that both are entirely in the retro frame.
- [The Brothers' War WPN product page](https://wpn.wizards.com/en/products/the-brothers-war?product=78cXoAUreNvjwDHELzldS6) supplies the official key art, logos, social assets, 24 × 36 poster, and oversized art downloads used in source selection.

The project inventory independently records The Brothers' War Commander as a complete `2/2` standalone release. No forced commander montage is used: the selected set-level WPN artwork more clearly and consistently identifies the associated product.

## Selection decision

The official 24 × 36 WPN poster is the strongest identity source: it is vertical, full bleed, unmistakably The Brothers' War, and has a clean central silhouette. The official social crops were kept in contention, but their integrated title sits too low to coexist with the standardized count seal after a 6:7 crop.

The initial v1 direct adaptation cropped the poster before its low title/footer and reapplied Wizards' supplied transparent logo higher on the cloak. Root QA correctly rejected that result because its bottom quarter remained a large low-information near-black cloak field. Both v1 root targets and their obsolete QA thumbnail were removed. The corrected v2 keeps the direct official upper/focal composition and exact official logo, but uses a built-in ImageGen precise-object edit to carry detailed machinery, debris, and forge glow through the lower edge. The generated lower region is feathered into the official source rather than used as a whole-image replacement.

## Production record

- Generation mode: built-in ImageGen precise-object edit for the lower battlefield only, followed by deterministic compositing with the official WPN source and official logo.
- Edit target: the rejected direct v1 base, created from the selected WPN poster crop and transparent official logo. The removed v1 base SHA-256 was `fda7421ba1b30ae137df3b25793235d66a9eb1bf76726180a0657416b5141582`.
- Exact edit prompt: preserved verbatim in `../prompts/brothers_war_commander_official_wpn_key_art_adaptation_brief.md`.
- Raw generator preservation: copied the built-in output unchanged to `brothers_war_commander_lower_battle_extension_imagegen_raw_v2.png`; 1162 × 1353 pixels, 8-bit RGB PNG.
- Poster anchor: Poppler `pdftoppm`, page 1, 75 PPI, produced the untouched 1800 × 2700 `bro_lgp_key_24x36_en_preview.png`. The direct official base uses crop `x=0, y=0, width=1800, height=2100`.
- Generator normalization: crop `x=1, y=0, width=1160, height=1353` from the raw output and scale to 1800 × 2100.
- Protected-region blend: preserve the direct official base unchanged above `y=1450`; feather from that base into the generator output across `y=1450–1780`; use the generated battlefield fully below `y=1780`. This protects the Magic wordmark, yellow diamond, war machines, central face, hands, and crystal while replacing the rejected cloak field with continuous machinery and forge texture.
- Exact-title lock: proportionally scale the official transparent 900 × 407 `MTGBRO_EN_SetLogo_1line.png` to 1640 × 742 and composite it last at `x=80, y=1120`. This guarantees exact official title glyphs and sharp edges regardless of any text drift in the generated edit.
- Base normalization: write the corrected v2 as non-interlaced 8-bit RGB PNG at exactly 1800 × 2100 pixels and set 600 × 600 DPI metadata.
- Count transform: create the corrected sibling non-destructively with `/usr/bin/swift scripts/apply_deck_count.swift ... 2:1800`. The approved 210-pixel seal occupies `x=1520–1730, y=1820–2030` in top-left pixel coordinates, leaving exactly 70 pixels at the right and bottom.

## Verification record

- Visually inspected the official poster, portrait social candidate, square social candidate, all four oversized-art alternatives, the raw ImageGen edit, corrected v2 unnumbered base, and corrected counted sibling.
- Inspected `brothers_war_commander_counted_qa_v2_360x420.png` at drawer-view scale; the title and `2` remain immediately readable, the central silhouette remains distinct, and the lower machinery still reads as useful battle detail.
- Exact dominant text appears once and reads `THE BROTHERS' WAR`; the official angular typographic apostrophe is clear and does not alter the phrase.
- The title is immediately legible, high contrast, unobstructed, and spans almost the full usable width.
- The Magic wordmark, central face, crystal, war machine, title, and count seal are all intact and comfortably separated.
- The seal covers only noncritical wrecked-machine detail and does not touch the title or a focal subject.
- Both finished files are exact 6:7, 1800 × 2100 pixels, with 600 × 600 DPI metadata. The base is 8-bit RGB; the counted sibling is 8-bit RGBA; both are non-interlaced PNGs.
- Artwork fills every edge. Shattered automata, gears, cables, sparks, furnace glow, and rubble create continuous meaningful detail throughout the full lower quarter and bottom edge; no flat, blank, featureless, or near-black footer remains.
