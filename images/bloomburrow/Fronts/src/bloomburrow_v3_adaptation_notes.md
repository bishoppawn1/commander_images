## Adaptation

No image generation was needed. The official portrait social asset already contains dense edge-to-edge artwork and a dominant, legible `BLOOMBURROW` title.

1. Center-crop the 1080 × 1920 source to 1080 × 1260, preserving the complete ensemble and integrated title while removing excess vertical framing.
2. Resize the exact 6:7 crop to 1800 × 2100 pixels.
3. Set horizontal and vertical resolution metadata to 600 DPI.

The original official image is retained unmodified beside the print target.
