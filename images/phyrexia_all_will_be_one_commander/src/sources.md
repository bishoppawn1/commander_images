# Phyrexia: All Will Be One Commander sources and production record

Downloaded and produced 2026-08-26. All remote sources are official Wizards of the Coast or Wizards Play Network resources.

## Product, debut, and deck-count verification

- [A First Look at Phyrexia: All Will Be One](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/a-first-look-at-phyrexia-all-will-be-one) identifies the associated set, its `ONE` set code and `ONC` Commander set code, the biomechanical New Phyrexia setting, and the two Commander products **Corrupting Influence** and **Rebellion Rising**. It credits the retained First Look key art to Anato Finnstark and the retained New Phyrexia landscape to Sergey Glushakov.
- [Where to Find Phyrexia: All Will Be One Previews](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/where-to-find-phyrexia-all-will-be-one-previews) is the official debut/previews guide. It directs viewers to the January 17 New Set Debut and the official card galleries.
- [Collecting Phyrexia: All Will Be One](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/collecting-phyrexia-all-will-be-one) is the debut-day product treatment overview. It states that two ready-to-play Commander decks arrive with the set and names **Corrupting Influence** and **Rebellion Rising**.
- [Phyrexia: All Will Be One Commander Decklists](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/phyrexia-all-will-be-one-commander-decklists) states that the release has two Commander decks, provides both complete decklists, and supplies the retained official package images.
- [Official Magic product page](https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/phyrexia-all-will-be-one) identifies the Commander product category and the set's Elesh Norn / biomechanical-zealot visual direction.
- [Official WPN product and marketing-material page](https://wpn.wizards.com/en/products/phyrexia-all-will-be-one) publishes the retained art/logo archive, 24 × 36 key-art poster, social-media archive, and oversized-art source.

These authoritative sources establish total ownership at **2/2**. The current physical drawer allocation represents **both** decks on the Commander Legends | Phyrexia: All Will Be One split face, whose right marker is `2`. Both preserved standalone visual candidates retain their original `2` counted siblings in the set root as alternate full-release targets; they are not an additional drawer allocation. Incorrect `1` siblings from the superseded allocation pass are retained only under `superseded_correction_history/`.

## Source-selection decision

The selected source is the official WPN 24 × 36 vertical key-art poster. It already provides the strongest possible associated-set identity: Elesh Norn framed by her porcelain headpiece and cathedral-like circles, exposed crimson biomechanical tissue, white armor, black-oil shadows, and detailed red cloth, all composed edge to edge. The portrait WPN social asset uses the same art family but has fewer source pixels; the First Look and oversized sources are horizontal. Because the official poster was already suitable, no ImageGen regeneration or two-face-commander montage was warranted.

The original poster lockup visually reads the stylized product logo but does not include the explicitly requested colon. The adaptation therefore crops away that separate logo panel and deterministically renders the exact drawer title `PHYREXIA: ALL WILL BE ONE` as `PHYREXIA:` / `ALL WILL BE ONE` over the continuing official illustration.

This removes the poster's separate lower title panel while preserving the small Magic mark, complete upper architecture and headpiece, Elesh Norn's head, hand, torso, armor, legs, red cloth, and lower biomechanical detail. The exact 6:7 crop is resampled without stretching to 1800 × 2100.
- Readability treatment: a shallow feathered oil-black/crimson veil sits over detailed lower official art; it does not create an opaque footer or empty field.
- Exact title: pale-ivory Copperplate Bold with dark outline and restrained crimson-black shadow, rendered once as `PHYREXIA:` / `ALL WILL BE ONE`. The upper line's visible glyph span is approximately x=77–1723 and the lower line's span is approximately x=104–1696, placing both near full usable width while keeping the punctuation and every glyph print-safe.
- Count marker: the original full-release sibling is active again in the set root, produced non-destructively with `swift scripts/apply_deck_count.swift … 2:1800`. The approved 210-pixel seal occupies x=1520–1730 and y=1820–2030 in top-left image coordinates, giving exactly 70 pixels of right and bottom outer-edge inset. The incorrect `1:1800` correction-pass sibling is archived under `superseded_correction_history/`.

## Secondary v2 candidate — native wordmark and tighter reframing

Produced 2026-08-26 as a non-destructive alternate after the original candidate's generic Copperplate title was judged insufficiently set-native. Both v1 finals above remain preserved without modification.

### Source comparison and selection

The secondary pass re-evaluated all retained official composition families at both full and reduced drawer scale:

- `secondary_layout_study_anato_center.png` is a 900 × 1050 central 6:7 study of the official Anato Finnstark First Look art. Its ritual symmetry and native embedded logo are very strong, but the landscape source provides only a 926 × 1080 portrait crop and its embedded subtitle occupies the mandated lower-right count-seal zone.
- `secondary_layout_study_matteo_center.png` is a 900 × 1050 central 6:7 study of the official Matteo Bassini oversized New Phyrexia architecture. It supplies excellent biomechanical scale and source resolution, but its bright pastel atmosphere is less oppressive and immediately character-identifiable than the Elesh Norn sources.
- `secondary_layout_study_poster_close.png` is a 900 × 1050 study of the selected tighter Magali Villeneuve poster crop. It preserves the highest-resolution vertical source, makes Elesh Norn substantially larger, fills every edge with the porcelain/red/oil-black visual language, and leaves a clean detailed lower field for the authentic transparent wordmark and count seal.
- `official_set_logo_on_black_preview.png` is a 900 × 407 inspection composite confirming the official transparent logo's bone/porcelain material, engraved outline, severe Phyrexian letterforms, and subtitle legibility before deterministic placement.

The selected secondary treatment therefore retains the Magali Villeneuve WPN poster but uses a materially tighter crop and the actual official WPN wordmark instead of v1's generic replacement font. Built-in ImageGen and CLI fallback were not used because the official high-resolution sources already supported a faithful, sharper deterministic result without character drift.
