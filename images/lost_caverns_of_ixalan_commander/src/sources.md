# The Lost Caverns of Ixalan Commander drawer-face sources and provenance

## Product verification and source-selection decision

The exact drawer-identification text is **`THE LOST CAVERNS OF IXALAN`**. The face represents the associated Commander release, but follows the project's main-set-first rule: official Lost Caverns key art and branding take priority over a montage of the preconstructed-deck commanders.

Authoritative Wizards sources establish the release and the four-deck count:

- **Wizards of the Coast — Collecting The Lost Caverns of Ixalan**  
  https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/collecting-the-lost-caverns-of-ixalan  
  Published October 24, 2023. The official product overview says there are four The Lost Caverns of Ixalan Commander decks and names **Ahoy Mateys**, **Blood Rites**, **Explorers of the Deep**, and **Veloci-Ramp-Tor**.
- **Wizards of the Coast — The Lost Caverns of Ixalan Commander Decklists**  
  https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/the-lost-caverns-of-ixalan-commander-decklists  
  Published November 1, 2023. The official decklists article independently describes four Commander decks releasing with the set.
- **Wizards Play Network — The Lost Caverns of Ixalan product page**  
  https://wpn.wizards.com/en/products/the-lost-caverns-of-ixalan  
  Authoritative source for the official Art and Logos, Key Art Social Media Assets, 24 x 36 Key Art Poster, Oversized Art, and Oversized Art Banner Stand downloads inspected below.
- **Wizards of the Coast — A First Look at The Lost Caverns of Ixalan**  
  https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/a-first-look-at-the-lost-caverns-of-ixalan  
  Published September 22, 2023. Official reveal/First Look source for the associated main-set identity, key art, set logo, product presentation, and LCI/LCC release relationship.
- **Wizards of the Coast — Where to Find The Lost Caverns of Ixalan Previews**  
  https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/where-to-find-the-lost-caverns-of-ixalan-previews  
  Published October 20, 2023. Official preview/debut hub confirming the October 24 debut and the set's artwork/reveal campaign.

These sources agree with `INVENTORY.md`: the owned drawer group is complete at 4/4, so the counted target uses one `4` seal.

The selected visual direction is the official WPN key-art social campaign and matching 24 x 36 poster featuring Huatli on a luminous cavern bat. It is the strongest existing branded vertical treatment: immediately recognizable, dense from edge to edge, and already designed around the exact English set logo. A small reference-led 6:7 extension was preferable to a Commander-face montage or an aggressive crop of the 9:16 social asset.

## Final transformations and targets

- Production helper: `build_target.swift`.
- Center-cropped only three source pixels of excess width from the built-in output, using the exact integer crop `x=1, y=0, width=1160, height=1353`; the full vertical extension is retained. The crop was then upscaled once with high-quality interpolation to exact 1800 x 2100.
- Added a transparent aubergine lower vignette over existing detailed cavern artwork for drawer-scale title contrast; no area was replaced by flat color.
- Cropped only transparent canvas padding from `lci_official_set_logo.png` using `x=2, y=136, width=1918, height=800`. The untouched official pixels were composited at x=70, bottom y=170, width=1450 px. The title therefore spans 80.6% of the full canvas and ends at x=1520, exactly where the count-seal zone begins.

## Final QA

- **Dimensions / print density:** both finished targets independently report exactly 1800 x 2100 pixels and 600 x 600 DPI through macOS ImageIO (`sips`).
- **Title accuracy:** the visible title comes from the untouched official high-resolution English set-logo pixels and reads exactly **THE LOST CAVERNS OF IXALAN**. It is a dominant two-line element with strong gold/orange/red contrast and no generated letters.
- **Drawer readability:** both targets were inspected at native resolution and at 300 x 350 drawer scale. All title words, Huatli, the central bat, Chimil glyph, and the `4` remain immediately recognizable.
- **Composition / density:** full bleed on all four sides; cavern cloud texture, Chimil light, mountains, structures, multiple distant bats, main wings, and foreground rock texture fill the frame. The dark title region retains visible cavern detail and is not a blank or flat field.
- **Subject integrity:** one central Huatli, one main cavern bat, one raised staff, and coherent paired wings; no duplicate main subject, stray text, watermark, package, card, or Commander montage.
- **Count seal:** the approved script's 210 px marker occupies x=1520..1730 and y=70..280 in bottom-origin coordinates, placing its outer edge exactly 70 px from both right and bottom. It covers only noncritical dark cavern texture and remains clear of both the title and focal subjects.
- **Folder hygiene:** the set root contains only the unnumbered and counted finished PNGs plus `prompts/` and `src/`. Official/raw downloads, unmodified ImageGen output, extracted references, helper script, QA previews, and provenance remain under `src/`.
