Key-art archive: https://media.wizards.com/2026/wpn/marketing_materials/trk/trk_sma_key_en.zip

The first generated front adapted the official five-captain key art. At the user's request, v2 removed the rightmost captain, leaving Sisko, Kirk, Picard and Janeway. The user subsequently requested Jonathan Archer, returning the portrait montage to five. Current v3 is an AI adaptation made with the built-in ImageGen tool, not untouched official artwork. Archer is depicted in his Enterprise-era uniform. Exact retained prompts are in ../prompts/; raw generations are preserved here.

Current full-bleed master: ../star_trek_five_captains_archer_v3.png, 1800 x 2100 at 600 DPI. Normalized mechanically using scripts/normalize_drawer_master.swift. Counted sibling has the standard four-deck seal applied by scripts/apply_deck_count.swift. Five portraits do not change the four-product deck count. The standard white-margin intermediate, crop, cut border and reframe pipeline produces the 1724 x 2024 black-margin master and 4138 x 4858 at 1440 DPI print sibling; both validators passed.

Front sheet 9 uses the counted v3 print sibling. Earlier generated variants are retained as sources. No Universes Beyond header is included; the title remains at the bottom.
