# The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth Commander source and production record

Downloaded and produced 2026-08-26. All external URLs below are official Wizards of the Coast or Wizards Play Network sources.

## Product, licensed identity, and deck-count verification

- [A First Look at The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/a-first-look-at-the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth) identifies the Universes Beyond set, shows the official key art, lists Commander decks among the June 2023 products, and provides the release schedule.
- [The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth WPN product page](https://wpn.wizards.com/en/products/the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth?product=BM5G5cs7uXHhuIYQmP9dM) supplies the official key art/logo archive, product shots, social assets, 24 × 36 poster, oversized art, and other retailer materials used in source selection.
- [The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth Commander Decklists](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth-commander-decklists) states that the release has **four new Commander decks** and names **Riders of Rohan**, **Food and Fellowship**, **Elven Council**, and **The Hosts of Mordor**. This verifies the drawer-face marker value `4`.
- [First Look for Retailers](https://wpn.wizards.com/en/news/first-look-for-retailers-the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth) independently establishes Wizards' licensed The Lord of the Rings set identity and highlights the One Ring, Mount Doom, and full-art Middle-earth material used in the release.
- Wizards' official pages carry the Middle-earth Enterprises licensing notice. The selected artwork and product identity are used as supplied official Wizards/WPN materials; no invented characters, franchise marks, or unlicensed replacement art were introduced.

The project inventory independently records The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth Commander as a complete `4/4` standalone release. Following the project's set-based Commander rule, the finished face uses the strongest official set-level branded art rather than forcing a montage of the four face commanders.

## Selection decision

The official 24 × 36 WPN poster is the strongest source. Its vertical licensed composition has the Magic and Universes Beyond marks, an unmistakable Frodo/Gollum split portrait, the One Ring, Mount Doom, foreground travelers, and edge-to-edge lava detail. It remains visually dense when cropped to 6:7 and requires no generative recreation.

The official 1080 × 1920 social portrait was kept in contention, but its extensive black headroom and footer violate the project's density requirement. The square and wide social assets need more extension and have less source resolution. The oversized art is landscape and less immediately branded. The Commander packaging is authoritative but would push the design toward a four-product/four-commander montage, contrary to the project's set-first selection rule.

The poster's original integrated title sits below the strongest 6:7 art region and would collide with the standardized lower-right count seal. The preserved v1 adaptation therefore uses the full upper poster crop and adds one deterministic exact-title plate above the seal zone. No ImageGen output is used: the official source already solves the artwork and identity requirements, and deterministic adaptation better preserves the licensed composition.

For the typography-focused secondary candidate, the artwork/crop selection remains unchanged and the v1 geometric sans/Copperplate plate is the only material design element replaced. The exact official set-title block from `MTGLTR_EN_SetLogo_LockUp.png` provides substantially stronger franchise-native engraved serif character, organic scale contrast, and official lockup spacing. Its source already contains the canonical `MIDDLE-EARTH` hyphen; two compact matching punctuation stops append the canonical colon omitted by the stylized official asset. A soft edge-fading black/oxblood ember veil provides local contrast without creating another modern text box.

## Production record — preserved v1

- Generation mode: no AI generation. Deterministic CoreGraphics/CoreText crop and title treatment over official WPN art.
- Production brief: `../prompts/lord_of_the_rings_tales_of_middle_earth_commander_official_wpn_poster_adaptation_brief.md`.
- Build script: `build_official_wpn_poster_target.swift`, retained in `src/` as a supporting production script.
- Poster rasterization: Poppler `pdftoppm`, page 1, 75 PPI, produced the untouched 1800 × 2700 `ltr_lgp_key_24x36_en_preview.png`.
- Crop: exact upper `x=0, y=0, width=1800, height=2100` pixels from the poster raster; no resampling of the licensed artwork.
- Title plate: rounded translucent oxblood-black plate at displayed `x=45, y=1490, width=1710, height=320`, with antique-gold outer stroke and inner rules. The underlying mountain/lava art remains visible through it.
- Exact-title lock: deterministic Copperplate Bold glyph rendering, pale antique-gold fill, deep-brown outline, and subtle shadow. First line `THE LORD OF THE RINGS:` and second line `TALES OF MIDDLE-EARTH`; each independently fitted inside `x=80–1720` to span nearly the full usable width.
- Base normalization: write an exact 1800 × 2100 non-interlaced 8-bit RGB PNG with 600 × 600 DPI metadata.
- Count transform: create the counted sibling non-destructively with `/usr/bin/swift scripts/apply_deck_count.swift ... 4:1800`.
- Marker geometry: the approved 210-pixel seal occupies `x=1520–1730, y=1820–2030` in top-left pixel coordinates, leaving exactly 70 pixels at the right and bottom outer edges.
