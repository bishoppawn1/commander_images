# Commander 2020 / Ikoria official-key-art adaptation brief

## Production mode

Direct, non-generative adaptation of official Wizards Play Network artwork. No ImageGen call is required because the official Ikoria key art already provides a strong, full-frame associated-set identity and a clear central subject.

## Input images and roles

- `../src/ikoria_official_key_art_poster_en_page1.png` — edit target and primary artwork. Preserve Taylor Ingvarsson's official composition, the central behemoth and hunter, the crystalline terrain, the full-frame painted texture, and the integrated `IKORIA: LAIR OF BEHEMOTHS` logo.
- `../src/ikoria_official_key_art_poster_en.pdf` — untouched official WPN source and provenance master for the rasterized page above.
- `../src/commander_2020_ruthless_regiment_product_reference.jpg` — product-identity reference only. It establishes the 2020 Commander packaging's red, black, ivory, and antique-gold visual language; do not composite the photographed package or its white background into the target.

## Final specification

Use case: precise-object-edit

Asset type: 3 by 3.5 inch portrait MTG drawer-identification face card

Primary request: adapt the official Ikoria key art to an edge-to-edge exact 6:7 portrait while preserving its central behemoth, human hunter, crystalline environment, and integrated Ikoria logo. Add one dominant title across the upper portion.

Text (verbatim): `COMMANDER 2020`

Typography: a single horizontal line of bold engraved fantasy display capitals, pale ivory with an antique-gold edge and a deep near-black outline/shadow. The exact title must span nearly the full usable width, remain inside an approximately 90-pixel print-safe inset at 1800-pixel width, and be immediately legible from drawer-viewing distance.

Composition/framing: crop the official landscape art around the behemoth's face and the hunter, shifting the crop slightly right to exclude the original upper-left Magic wordmark cleanly while retaining the complete official Ikoria logo near the bottom. Use the entire canvas; keep artwork and meaningful texture touching all four edges. Add only a translucent dark top gradient behind the title so the painted sky and spikes remain visible.

Output: unnumbered 1800 by 2100 pixel sRGB PNG with 600-DPI metadata. Preserve this base unchanged, then create a separately named counted sibling with the approved single `5` seal at right boundary 1800 and equal 70-pixel right/bottom insets. The standalone drawer displays all five owned Commander 2020 / Ikoria decks.

Constraints: preserve the official artwork without generative alteration; exact 6:7 portrait; no blank, white, featureless, or empty areas; no commander montage; no deck names; no added characters; no additional copy; no malformed lettering; no watermark; no decorative frame; no title obstruction; keep the lower-right area usable for the approved seal.
