# Boulder-top composition brief

Use this directive for every new top-label prompt and final composition. The art canvas is 33:28 landscape; the final top is 3960 x 3360 at 1440 DPI (2.75 x 2 1/3 inches).

"Keep the focal face near the center of the artwork. Reserve the upper 18% for a horizontally centered mana-symbol row. Place the complete focal head typically 22–52% down the canvas, with comfortable space around hair, ears, horns, crowns and hats. Keep heads above the lower title area and fully inside the cut border. Leave atmospheric scenery behind the top symbols and lower typography. Art-only output has no text, logos or symbols. Preserve character identity and anatomy."

The mana row belongs at top center by default. Reframe a high portrait instead of shifting the mana row sideways to avoid it. The shared builder preserves approved artwork and allows explicit per-label placement/scale overrides. It does not attempt automatic face detection or change every label indiscriminately.

Artwork must continue from top to bottom inside the decorative/cut frame. When lowering or shrinking retained artwork leaves an exposed black band or empty canvas, generate an extension of the scene above it (and at any other exposed edge). The upper mana area must contain continuous atmospheric artwork, not a flat black header. Outpaint the missing background while preserving the existing characters and their approved positions; retain crisp text and symbols through the deterministic compositor. Never stretch, mirror or repeat a thin strip of background to fill the gap. Inspect the final composite for continuous coverage and visible seams. Provide before/after rows for every affected label, preserving the earlier files.

For corrections, inspect every selected final label and compare failed cases with retained source art. Keep compliant files byte-for-byte unchanged. Fix placement or scale first; use generation only if missing source content prevents a satisfactory composition. Save corrected outputs with a versioned outputStem and retain the old output. Check full heads, centered mana, legible titles, edge clearance and printed physical size in the final rendered PDF. Record pass/fix decisions for the whole inventory.
